Core Defense Engines

Aggressive Popup & Popunder Blocker Traps unrequested window spawns, synthetic anchor click exploits, and background window refocusing.
Anti-Redirect & Tab-Under Hijack Shield Neutralizes timer-based and tab-under redirects (location.replace, meta refresh, synthetic links) preventing unwanted site navigation.
Tracking & Telemetry Armor Blocks cross-site trackers, ad telemetry pixels, analytics beacons, and behavioral profiling networks.
URL Tracking Parameter Stripper Automatically cleans query tracking tokens (utm_*, fbclid, gclid, mc_cid, etc.) from clicked links and URLs.
Canvas & Audio Fingerprint Randomizer Injects imperceptible deterministic noise into canvas and AudioContext APIs to defeat device fingerprinting.
WebRTC IP Leak Defense Eliminates WebRTC local/public IP address disclosure on non-proxied UDP connections.
Cookie Consent Banner Auto-Dismissal Automatically dismisses or hides intrusive GDPR/CCPA cookie consent banners without accepting tracking.
Anti-Adblock Defuser Neutralizes anti-adblock traps and provides safe dummy variables to prevent page breakage.
Video Pre-roll & VAST Defuser (Fix Error 224003) Neutralizes broken video prerolls, skips VAST ad crashes (Error Code 224003), and ensures smooth playback on JWPlayer and HTML5 players.
Popup Blocked Toast Notifications Displays a subtle floating badge at the bottom corner when a popup is intercepted with 1-click allow/whitelist.
